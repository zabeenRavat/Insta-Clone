# Insta-Clone

**Home-Manage** is a web-based application designed to simplify household management by bringing daily home tasks and financial records together in one place.

Managing a home involves tracking expenses, organizing chores, remembering important tasks, and maintaining household budgets. Home-Manage provides an easy-to-use platform that helps families and individuals stay organized and in control of their daily responsibilities.

## Features

- 🏠 Manage daily, weekly, and monthly household tasks.
- 💰 Record and track household income and expenses.
- 📊 View financial summaries and spending reports.
- ✅ Create and monitor to-do lists.
- 📅 Schedule important household activities and reminders.
- 🧾 Maintain records of bills and recurring payments.
- 📂 Organize household information in one central location.
- 🔍 Search and filter tasks and financial records easily.

## Purpose

The goal of Home-Manage is to reduce the stress of household management by providing a simple and organized system for handling both home activities and finances. Whether you're managing a family home or your personal household, Home-Manage helps keep everything organized and accessible.

## Target Users

- Families
- Individuals living independently
- Homemakers
- Students managing shared accommodation
- Anyone looking for an easy way to organize household tasks and finances
